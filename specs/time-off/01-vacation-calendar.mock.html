Time off calendar — visual acceptance target

A horizontal wallchart: one row per member, one column per day. Bands are vacation requests — solid where approved, hatched where pending; rejected and cancelled are never drawn. Weekend columns take --surface-sunken. A public holiday that applies to every member in view shades the whole column (21 Sep, "Company Day"); one that applies to some of them marks only their cells and gets a hollow dot in the header (16 Sep, "Polish National Day", PL only). The three scopes are the same grid over a different row set.

The band colour, the pinned column's width and the 3px that separates two adjacent bands are what this screen asks the design system for — see the DS gaps table in the design notes. The scope and window segments are ToggleButton, drawn here as a stand-in.

Every active member, September 2026. Olga Admin has no absence: the row still draws, because "nobody is away" is the answer the chart exists to give.
