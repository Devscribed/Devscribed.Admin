consent/01 — Cookies and storage

The consent dialog, on the public surfaces only: /book/{slug}, /manage/{slug}/{token}, /sign/{token} and the six signed-out account screens. Two categories, because two is what the product actually writes: the ds_session cookie, and the one preference key in localStorage.

The backdrop is the booking page, chosen deliberately — the 24h/12h control in Pick a time is the functional storage this dialog is asking about, so the thing at stake is visible behind the thing asking.

1 · First visit — no decision recorded

There is no ×, Escape does nothing, and clicking the scrim does nothing. A dialog asking for consent must not have a way out that records no answer — and that is the one thing the design system's Modal cannot do today.

3 · Reopened from the footer — accepted twelve months ago

The same dialog and the same two buttons, with two differences, each because a decision already exists: a line states what is on now and when it was chosen, and there is a × — leaving without changing anything is a real answer once an answer is on file. The current setting is stated in prose and in the category row, never by changing a button's label: a control whose text depends on state is a control that moves.
