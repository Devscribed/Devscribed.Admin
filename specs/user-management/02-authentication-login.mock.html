02 — Authentication & Login

Visual acceptance target for /login, /forgot-password and /reset-password, on the Teammerly Original DS. Every state of all three screens on one page. Pairs with 02-authentication-login.design.md; the numbered §n references there point at specs/design-system/ledger.md.

Two changes here are reversals rather than repaints, and both are visible below. The login banner no longer swaps to amber for a deactivated account — blue paints one banner for anything that went wrong, and the wording was always what carried the meaning. And the checking state is three pulsing dots, not a sizeable arc: blue's Preloader is production's PulseLoader, whose size is the dot, not the widget.
