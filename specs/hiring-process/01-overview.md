# Hiring Process

## Summary

Our hiring process consists our of multiple steps that we perform one by one until actual hiring happens: 

1. The interview booking

Candidates are using our booking pages to book an interview. Each booking creates an invite in our MS 365 Office calendar. See 02-booking-page/booking-page.md for details.

2. The interview process

The interview happens and scheduled time. Each candidate is part of our hiring database and we leave comments and notes for each candidate during the interview.

3. Offer Letter

If we like the candidate, we send our offer letter to the candidate.

4. The Onboarding Checklist

If the candidate accepts, we need to start the onboarding process and add the candidate in our system: MS Office, Slack, etc. We send the agreement for a signature using Adobe Sign. We also sign a few other documents.
